Jodo Drive - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

What the app is

Jodo Drive is a personal app that lets its owner see and manage files across the owner's own Google Drive, OneDrive and Dropbox accounts in one place.

What access it asks for

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to see, create, move and delete files in your Google Drive, and to read your email address and name so it can show which account is connected. The app uses this access only to do what you ask inside the app (list files, upload, move, delete, show thumbnails, find duplicates).

What happens to your data

  • Your files, photos and account details are not sent to any server run by the app's owner. There is no such server.
  • Sign-in tokens and settings are stored only on your own device (in the app's local storage).
  • Photo categories (people, food, nature and so on) are worked out on your device by small AI models. Photos are not uploaded anywhere for this.
  • The Vault feature encrypts photos on your device with a password only you know before they are stored in your cloud account. The owner cannot recover a forgotten Vault password.
  • The app does not sell, share or use your data for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jodo Drive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and you can sign out or remove an account inside the app.
